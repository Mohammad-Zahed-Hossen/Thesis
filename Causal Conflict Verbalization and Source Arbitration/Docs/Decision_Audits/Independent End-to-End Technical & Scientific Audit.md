## 1. Executive Snapshot

Your thesis has evolved from a flawed v3 prompt-comparison study into a methodologically sophisticated v5 factorial prefix-forcing design (*Does the Verdict Decide?*) backed by a well-engineered Python/PyTorch harness (`196/196` unit tests passing) and a completed 29,880-row `bfloat16` dev pass on an NVIDIA L4 GPU across three Qwen-VL models (`2B`, `3B`, `7B`) and two conflict regimes (`Math` task-selection conflict and `ChartQA` evidence conflict) `[Verified]`. **Your single biggest strength** is the scientific honesty and rigor of your self-correction: you caught the v3 instruction-vs-verbalization confound (`repetition_penalty`, truncation, and `last_num` scoring bugs), isolated instruction dominance in v4 dev (`+20.9 pp` under neutral vs. `-1.8 pp` under conflict on $N=110$ dual-competent items), and built a teacher-forced log-likelihood margin ($\Delta\ell$) pipeline with zero token-boundary mismatches across all `20,868` scored dev rows `[Verified]`. **Your single biggest blocker** is that you **cannot freeze the pre-registration or run confirmatory data yet** because the v5 dev pass exposed severe construct-validity, parser/splitter, and sample-retention failures that interact with at least seven code/analysis bugs discovered in this audit—most critically: (1) on Math, the teacher-forced margin $\Delta\ell$ at `####` (0 CoT tokens) is decoupled from 512-token behavioural CoT solving (`51.6%` sign agreement on 3B, `56.5%` on 7B), and `93.6%` of 7B's apparent binding $\text{CDE}$ (`+1.5154` of `+1.6188`) comes simply from deleting the model's own verdict (`none - own`) rather than following the flipped verdict (`flip - none = +0.1033`); (2) `Qwen2-VL-2B-Instruct` has `0/200` Math competency (`0` `####` tokens emitted) and `0/10` ChartQA edit-gate survival; (3) ChartQA retains only `6/30` (3B) and `2/30` (7B) items and leaks the numerical answer in Sentence 1 of the prefill in `50.0%` (3B) and `68.3%` (7B) of donor verbalizations; and (4) `analyze_v5.py` has a Holm-Bonferroni family-size bug ($m=5$ instead of $m=3$), a `change_rate` bug that counts `None` (`"neither"`) transitions as source flips (inflating `own`-vs-`para` wording churn from `1.88%`/`0.00%` to `25.6%`/`20.4%`), and a Math behavioural `none` prefill (`{m_none}\n\n` without `####`) that causes models to spontaneously generate a new reliability verdict in `69.4%` (3B) and `88.5%` (7B) of "no-verdict" behavioural trials `[Verified]`.

### 2.2 Current `v5` Research Question, Causal Estimand & Claim Boundaries

- **Research Question ([PREREGISTRATION_VERDICT_TRANSPLANT.md](file:///d:/Thesis/Important/Causal%20Conflict%20Verbalization%20and%20Source%20Arbitration/Docs/PREREGISTRATION_VERDICT_TRANSPLANT.md#L54-L60)):** Under image–text conflict, on items where a VLM can solve both sources in isolation, does prefix-forcing a minimal-edit flip of the model's own stated reliability verdict causally steer its commitment toward the named source's answer (`non-binding` vs. `binding`), and what fraction of the explicit single-source instruction ceiling does the verdict recover `[Verified]`?
- **Primary Outcome ($\Delta\ell$, [PREREGISTRATION_VERDICT_TRANSPLANT.md](file:///d:/Thesis/Important/Causal%20Conflict%20Verbalization%20and%20Source%20Arbitration/Docs/PREREGISTRATION_VERDICT_TRANSPLANT.md#L280-L289)):** Length-normalized teacher-forced log-likelihood margin immediately after `{m}\n\n####` (image minus text):
  $$
  \Delta\ell(x, b, k, o, m) = \frac{1}{|y_I|}\log p_{\theta}(y_I \mid x, I_{b,k}, o, m, \text{\texttt{####}}) - \frac{1}{|y_T|}\log p_{\theta}(y_T \mid x, I_{b,k}, o, m, \text{\texttt{####}})

  $$

  where orientation $s_{i,o} \in \{+1, -1\}$ is set **per item and evidence order** ($s_{i,o} = +1$ if $m_{\text{flip}}$ names the image/chart; $s_{i,o} = -1$ if $m_{\text{flip}}$ names the text/report) `[Verified]`.
- **Three Primary Estimands per Model $\times$ Regime ([PREREGISTRATION_VERDICT_TRANSPLANT.md](file:///d:/Thesis/Important/Causal%20Conflict%20Verbalization%20and%20Source%20Arbitration/Docs/PREREGISTRATION_VERDICT_TRANSPLANT.md#L308-L335)):**
  1. **Oriented Controlled Direct Effect ($\text{CDE}_b$):** $\text{CDE}_{i,b} = \frac{1}{8}\sum_{k=1}^{4}\sum_{o \in \{-0.5, +0.5\}} s_{i,o}\big[\Delta\ell(x_i, b, k, o, m_{i,o}^{\text{flip}}) - \Delta\ell(x_i, b, k, o, m_{i,o}^{\text{own}})\big]$ for $b \in \{\text{nonbind}, \text{bind}\}$ `[Verified]`.
  2. **Binding Interaction ($T_1$, Difference-in-Differences):** $\text{DiD}_i = \text{CDE}_{i,\text{bind}} - \text{CDE}_{i,\text{nonbind}}$, tested via one-sided paired sign-flip permutation test ($B=10{,}000$, seed `42`) `[Verified]`.
  3. **Ceiling-Normalized Recovery Ratios ($T_2 = \rho_{\text{bind}}$, $T_3 = \rho_{\text{nonbind}}$):** $\rho_b = \overline{\text{CDE}}_b / \overline{\text{Ceil}}_\ell$, where $\text{Ceil}_{\ell,i} = \frac{1}{8}\sum_{k,o}\big[\Delta\ell(x_i, \text{explicit\_image}, k, o, m_{i,o}^{\varnothing}) - \Delta\ell(x_i, \text{explicit\_text}, k, o, m_{i,o}^{\varnothing})\big]$, tested against the region of practical equivalence $[-0.25, +0.25]$ via 90% paired item-bootstrap CIs ($B=10{,}000$) when the 95% CI of $\overline{\text{Ceil}}_\ell$ excludes `0` `[Verified]`.
- **Mandatory Claim Boundaries ([PREREGISTRATION_VERDICT_TRANSPLANT.md](file:///d:/Thesis/Important/Causal%20Conflict%20Verbalization%20and%20Source%20Arbitration/Docs/PREREGISTRATION_VERDICT_TRANSPLANT.md#L35-L49)):**
  - Prefix-forcing identifies a **Controlled Direct Effect (CDE)** under external suffix substitution, **not** the Natural Indirect Effect (NIE / statistical mediation) of free chain-of-thought generation `[Verified]`.
  - No claims of decoupled internal neural circuits (no layer probing/patching is performed) `[Verified]`.
  - Claims are scoped per individual model (`Qwen2-VL-2B`, `Qwen2.5-VL-3B`, `Qwen2.5-VL-7B`), never generalized to all VLMs `[Verified]`.

## 5. What Is Wrong (Bugs, Blockers, Methodological & Operational Risks)

### 5.1 Critical Pre-Freeze Blockers (Must Be Resolved Before Freezing `prereg-v5` or Running Confirmatory Data)

1. **Blocker 1 — Decoupling Between Primary Outcome $\Delta\ell$ (`0` CoT Tokens) and Behavioural Solving (`512` CoT Tokens) on Math (`Construct Validity`) `[Verified]`:**

   - Across all attributable behavioural rows (`src in {"image", "text"}`) at $k=1$, the sign of the teacher-forced margin $\Delta\ell$ agrees with the source of the model's generated behavioural answer on only **`545 / 1,057 (51.56%)`** of rows in **3B Math** (chance is `50%`!) and **`582 / 1,030 (56.50%)`** (`583/1,030 = 56.60%` if $\Delta\ell \le 0$ counts as text) in **7B Math** `[Verified]`.
   - **Why:** $\Delta\ell$ probes the log-likelihood of immediately emitting the final number $y_I$ vs. $y_T$ right after `{m}\n\n####` **with zero chain-of-thought calculation steps**! Because GSM8K/SVAMP word problems require multi-step arithmetic, at `####` before any calculation is written, the model has not computed the answer to either word problem—its logits over `y_I` vs. `y_T` are dominated by surface number copying and evidence-order recency (`IF` mean $\Delta\ell$ in 3B is `-0.81` to `-0.88`; `TF` mean $\Delta\ell$ is `+0.46` to `+0.87`) `[Verified]`. By contrast, on ChartQA (where no multi-step calculation is needed and `beh` is also prefilled with `{m}\n\n####`), $\text{sign}(\Delta\ell)$ agrees with the behavioural answer on **`108 / 109 (99.08%)`** of 3B rows and **`47 / 48 (97.92%)`** of 7B rows `[Verified]`.
2. **Blocker 2 — 7B Math $\text{CDE}$ Is `93.6%` Driven by Deleting the Model's Own Verdict (`none - own`), Not by Following the Flipped Verdict (`flip - none`), and Produces $\hat\rho_{\text{bind}} > 1$ `[Verified]`:**

   - In 7B Math ($N=115$), decomposing $\widehat{\text{CDE}}_{\text{bind}} = +1.6188$ into `Decomp1 = none - own` (removing Sentence 2) and `Decomp2 = flip - none` (inserting the flipped verdict) reveals that **`+1.5154` (`93.6%`) already happens when the reliability sentence is simply removed (`none - own`)**, while replacing `none` with the flipped verdict (`flip - none`) adds only **`+0.1033` (`6.4%`)**—and in `text_first`, `flip - none` is **negative (`-0.2417`)** (`none` has a higher image margin `+1.0766` than `flip` `+0.8349`!) `[Verified]`.
   - In 3B Math ($N=80$), the explicit-image ceiling direction (`explicit_image - nonbinding|none`) is **`-0.0087` (`95% CI [-0.0408, +0.0206]`, indistinguishable from zero)**, compressing the denominator $\overline{\text{Ceil}}_\ell = +0.1524$ so that $\hat\rho_{\text{bind}} = 1.5291$ (`153%` of ceiling) `[Verified]`.
3. **Blocker 3 — Complete Collapse of `Qwen2-VL-2B-Instruct` (`0/200` Math Competent, `0/10` ChartQA Gate-Passing) `[Verified]`:**
