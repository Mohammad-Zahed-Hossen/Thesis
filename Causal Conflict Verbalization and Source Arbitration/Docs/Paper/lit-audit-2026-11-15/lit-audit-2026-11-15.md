**Top 5 design implications** (detail in §4; none requires a new GPU run before the freeze):
1. **Keep the order counterbalancing; expect a large order effect.** *Same Semantics* (2609.00550, Table 1, p. 5) uses the same format as our Math stimuli (a rendered image of a passage against the typed passage). Its order effect is large in Qwen2.5-VL-7B: the image-over-text gap is −68.98 with image first and +73.82 with text first, so the later source wins. Pre-specify how the order-averaged CDE is read if the two per-order CDEs differ in sign.
2. **D1 has independent support.** In Qwen2.5-VL-7B/3B, reading answer logits right after a reasoning cue measures the wrong event (2609.29278, p. 1). Cite this paper for keeping Δℓ secondary in Math.
3. **Add a limitation on reading H₀.** Models can resist a prefill that opposes their own preference and revert toward baseline (2606.12747, p. 1). A null `flip − own` could therefore be prefill resistance rather than "saying is not choosing". Report the CDE stratified by `verdict_nll(flip) − verdict_nll(own)`, a variable already recorded, as a descriptive check.
4. **Treat binding as a weak lever.** An instruction to verify a trace in the model's own thinking block did not loosen its hold: trace-following was 73.6% under the instruction versus 72.2% without it (2610.20805, Table 3, p. 7). The §7.5 manipulation check is essential before a null T1 is read.
5. **Narrow the claims.**
   - Competency conditioning defines a sub-population (2610.37070, p. 1); add that as a limitation.
   - In Math, the "image" is rendered text. Results are about *rendered-problem images vs. typed text*, not visual evidence in general (2609.00550; 2611.01517; 2609.06011).

### 1.2 What survives

Within the 1,260 records screened, no study does all of the following together:
- intervenes on a **VLM's own stated source-reliability verdict** by assistant-turn prefix-forcing a minimally edited version;
- uses a matched template pair as control;
- measures which source the answer follows under **image–text** conflict with counterbalanced order;
- tests whether a **binding instruction** changes that effect.

The closest designs split the components:
- 2610.30953 has the edit design (own-reasoning edit, paraphrase-subtracted contrast, re-injection check), but in driving VLAs with a trajectory outcome and no source conflict.
- 2610.20805 has a trace in the model's own turn under conflict, plus an instruction contrast, but it is text-only and has no verdict.
- 2609.00550 has order-counterbalanced image–text (rendered passage) conflict, but no verbalization.

## 2. A-paper spec sheets

### A1. Same Semantics, Different Outcome — arXiv:2609.00550
- **Citation and access.** Jungyeon Lee, Yejin Yoon, Taeuk Kim (Hanyang Univ.). *Findings of EMNLP 2026* per OpenAlex (DOI `10.18653/2026.findings-emnlp.693`). https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2609.00550 — [Full text read: abstract, §3–4, Table 1]. PDF: `Docs/Paper/2609.00550.pdf` (20 pp.).
- **Task and stimuli.** ConflictQA and NQ-Swap. A context passage that contradicts parametric knowledge is given as text, as a **rendered image of the same passage**, or as both. The multi-evidence setting gives an image passage and a text passage that support competing answers. [Verified, §3, p. 4]
- **Models.** 13 MLLMs, including **Qwen2.5-VL-7B/-32B**, InternVL3, LLaVA, Qwen2.5/3-Omni, GPT-4o, GPT-5.4 and Claude Sonnet 4.5. [Verified, Table 1, p. 5]
- **Manipulation and order.**
  - The form of the evidence is varied, and in the joint setting the input order (I→T vs. T→I) is varied.
  - Order is run in both directions: x_{I→T} = (e_I, e_T, q) and x_{T→I} = (e_T, e_I, q). The question comes last in both. [Verified, p. 4]
- **Outcome and key result.**
  - Measure: the normalized image-over-text gap G ∈ [−100, 100].
  - Qwen2.5-VL-7B on ConflictQA: G = **−68.98** for I→T and **+73.82** for T→I. The later source wins.
  - 8 of 13 models (ConflictQA) and 6 of 13 (NQ-Swap) change the sign of G with order. [Verified, Table 1, p. 5; §4]
- **Statistics.** Descriptive rates. No CIs were seen in the sections read. [Verified for those sections]
- **Novelty threat.** (a) none; (b) partial; (c) none.
- **Design implication.** Strong support for Invariant 1. It predicts a large order main effect in our stimuli for Qwen2.5-VL-7B (§4 row O). Our instruction stays before both sources, unlike their question-last layout, so the size of the effect may differ. [Inferred]
- **Quote.** "when a contradicting text and image are presented together, the preferred modality is essentially arbitrary, varying with input order, model, and dataset" (p. 1).

### A2. Measuring the Reasoning–Action Coupling in Driving VLAs through Counterfactuals — arXiv:2610.30953
- **Citation and access.** Gabriel Manalu, Saad Wazir, Thao Do, Seongah Kim, et al. (KAIST), arXiv 2026-10. [Full text read: abstract, §1, §3, Table 1]. PDF: `Docs/Paper/2610.30953.pdf` (11 pp.).
- **Task.** Four driving vision-language-action models on driving benchmarks. The outcome is trajectory displacement (ADE). [Verified, abstract, p. 1]
- **Manipulation.**
  - Reasoning side: the model's **own generated reasoning** is edited and fed back at trajectory decoding. The edits are FILLER, DELETE, SWAP (graft another scene's decision sentence), MISTAKE (LLM rewrite of the decision sentence) and NEGATE (template negation of the decision sentence).
  - Each content edit is contrasted against a meaning-preserving PARAPHRASE: Δ = d_T − d_paraphrase.
  - A check re-injects the model's own unedited reasoning through the same path. [Verified, Fig. 1 and §3, pp. 1, 3, 6]
  - Image side: key objects named in the reasoning are removed photorealistically, and the model is rerun freely.
- **Conflict and order.** No image–text source conflict. Order is not applicable.
- **Key result.** Altering the reasoning's meaning produces small trajectory changes relative to blanking the camera inputs (abstract, p. 1). The effect is model-dependent (p. 7):
  - For AutoVLA and ORION, MISTAKE is indistinguishable from the PARAPHRASE null.
  - For Alpamayo-R1, SWAP and NEGATE stay below 3.20% of the camera-side ADE response.
  - For Alpamayo-1.5, content edits reach 14.48–19.46% of the camera-side response.
  [Verified, p. 1 and p. 7]
- **Statistics.** Three seeds; comparisons paired within scene and seed. [Verified, p. 6]
- **Novelty threat.** (a) partial; (b) none; (c) none.
- **Design implication.** This is the closest published precedent for our `flip` / `para` / `own`-replay structure, so cite it in §1.1 and Ch. 2. Its NEGATE edit is a template edit of the decision sentence, analogous to our noun-phrase swap. Its re-injection check is analogous to our replay-noise measure (§7.6). [Inferred]
- **Quote.** "we use a paired contrast that subtracts, for each scene, the response to a meaning-preserving PARAPHRASE" (p. 3).

### A3. When Reasoning Traces Conflict with Evidence — arXiv:2610.20805
- **Citation and access.** Shaoang Li, Yanhang Shi, Jian Li (Stony Brook), arXiv 2026-10 (v2). [Full text read: abstract, §1–3, §5, Tables 1–3]. PDF: `Docs/Paper/2610.20805.pdf` (25 pp.).
- **Task and models.** HotpotQA (683 verified pairs) and MuSiQue (417). Models: gpt-oss-20b, Gemma4-E4B, Qwen3 (4B–32B), Qwen3.5-4B, DeepSeek-V4-Pro (text-only). [Verified, §2.2, Table 1, p. 2]
- **Manipulation.**
  - A trace r₁ generated from edited evidence E₁ is paired with the original evidence E₀ at answer time.
  - It is answered either by **continuation**, with the trace inserted into the model's own thinking block and the block closed, or by a **separate answer call**, with the trace supplied in the user message.
  - Two corrections are tested: "Verify", an instruction to check the trace against the evidence, and "Re-reason". [Verified, §2.1, §5.1, pp. 2, 7]
- **Conflict and order.** Trace vs. evidence (text–text). Input order is varied in an appendix ablation. [Verified, §5.2, p. 7]
- **Key results.**
  - Continuation repeats the stale trace conclusion in 65.6–87.0% of crossed answers (§3.3, p. 5).
  - A separate call raises evidence-following by 29.9–60.6 points (Table 2, p. 5).
  - Verify leaves trace-following at 73.6% vs. 72.2% for continuation; Re-reason follows the evidence in 86.6% (Table 3, p. 7).
  - Putting the trace last in the separate call cuts evidence-following by 27.2–36.6 points.
  - Labelling the trace as the model's own reasoning does not raise reliance (§5.2, pp. 7–8). [Verified]
- **Statistics.** Pair-level 95% bootstrap intervals. [Verified, §1, p. 2]
- **Novelty threat.** (a) partial; (b) none; (c) partial. Formally a threat (see §1).
- **Design implications.** [Inferred]
  1. Content placed in the model's own turn is followed strongly, so `own` and `flip` must sit at identical positions. They do (both are prefills), so the contrast is not confounded by position.
  2. An instruction about how to treat that content may barely move behaviour. This sets a low prior for T1 and makes the §7.5 manipulation check essential.
- **Quote.** "The verification instruction we tested does not loosen the hold of the trace" (p. 7).

### A4. Reasoning Instructions Can Break Answer Decoding in VLMs — arXiv:2609.29278
- **Citation and access.** Zeyan Li, Siyuan Qiu, Jianfeng Xu (SJTU), arXiv 2026-09. [Full text read: whole paper]. PDF: `Docs/Paper/2609.29278.pdf` (5 pp.).
- **Task and models.**
  - ScienceQA, AI2D, MMMU-10 and MMBench.
  - Models: **Qwen2.5-VL-7B** and **Qwen2.5-VL-3B**, plus LLaVA-1.5-7B. [Verified, Table 1, p. 2]
- **Manipulation.** Appending "Let me think step by step." and reading answer-label logits at the next position (CoT-prefix scoring), compared with direct scoring and with free CoT. [Verified, §2, p. 2]
- **Key results.**
  - Qwen2.5-VL-7B on ScienceQA: 80.76% → **45.48%** under CoT-prefix scoring.
  - First-slot rate: 93.54% across five option permutations.
  - Probes recover 78.94%, and free generation restores 75.24%.
  - AI2D Qwen2.5-VL-7B: −54.02 points. Not universal: MMBench +5.80. [Verified, pp. 1–2, Table 1]
- **Novelty threat.** None on (a), (b) or (c).
- **Design implication.** This is the same failure as our Math Δℓ at `####`: a reasoning-requesting context with the answer scored before any reasoning. It is independent, published evidence for D1, the demotion of Δℓ to secondary in Math. Keep ChartQA Δℓ only because its templates are answer-only (16-token decode per `Experiment/README.md`). [Inferred]
- **Quote.** "CoT-prefix scoring can confound model knowledge with an evaluation-interface mismatch and should be avoided unless the requested and scored output events are aligned" (p. 1).

### A5. Prefill Awareness in Large Language Models — arXiv:2606.12747
- **Citation and access.** Andy Wang, Parv Mahajan, David Demitri Africa, Alexandra Souly, Jordan Taylor, Robert Kirk (Constellation / UK AISI), arXiv 2026-06. [Full text read: abstract, §1, results summary; 68 pp., the appendix was not read]. PDF: `Docs/Paper/2606.12747.pdf`.
- **Task and models.**
  - A binary-preference benchmark filtered for consistent stances.
  - Three prefill mechanisms: thinking-trace tamper, direct-answer tamper and past-round tamper.
  - Frontier models plus some open-weight models (e.g. Gemma 3 27B, Qwen3-coder, Llama 3.1 70B). Text-only. [Verified, p. 1–2; model list from tables]
- **Key results.**
  - Claude Opus 4.5 detects prefills opposing its preferences in 9–35% of cases with 0% false positives when prompted.
  - Models often **revert toward baseline** without reporting the prefill.
  - Preference mismatch mainly drives reversion; stylistic mismatch mainly drives flagging. [Verified, p. 1]
- **Novelty threat.** None.
- **Design implication.** A minimally edited flip keeps the style but opposes the model's own preference, which is the condition that drives reversion here. So an H₀ reading ("saying is not choosing") has a competing explanation, *prefill resistance*. This is a limitation to state, and it adds a descriptive analysis (§4 row M). The findings come from text-only frontier models; transfer to 3B/7B VLMs is [Unknown].
- **Quote.** "models often revert towards baseline behavior without explicitly reporting that the prefill was foreign" (p. 1).

### A6. Correctness Conditioning as Selection in CoT Faithfulness Evaluation — arXiv:2610.37070
- **Citation and access.** Yueer Pan, Yuexi Pan, arXiv 2026-10. [Full text read: abstract, §1–2]. PDF: `Docs/Paper/2610.37070.pdf` (28 pp.).
- **Content.**
  - Builds a seven-variable SCM of correctness selection, with observed gap Δ = Cov(A, C)/P(C=1).
  - Reviews the protocols of 18 papers.
  - Primary GLM-5.3 run on arithmetic DAGs: **+7.4 pp** selection gap (n = 1,000, p < 0.0001); pooled CMH OR = 1.70. [Verified, p. 1]
- **Novelty threat.** None.
- **Design implication.** Our competency filter is applied in separate single-source arms, not on the outcome arm. It is therefore a pre-treatment covariate and does not open their collider path for the `flip − own` contrast. It still defines a sub-population (items solvable from each source alone), so the estimand is conditional. Add a limitation, and report the pass rate per model. [Inferred]
- **Quote.** "Restricting chain-of-thought (CoT) faithfulness evaluation to correctly answered instances changes the population being measured." (p. 1).
