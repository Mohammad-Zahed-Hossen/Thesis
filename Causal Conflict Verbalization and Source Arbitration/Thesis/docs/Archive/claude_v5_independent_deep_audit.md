## 10. Methodological risks and unresolved pre-freeze decisions

Each remedy listed here is a **pre-freeze decision that needs the researcher's approval**. None is authorized by this audit.

### 10.1 Interpretation

1. **Technical feasibility: [Verified] demonstrated** for the scoring pipeline on an L4/bf16 with batch size 2 for 7B, with zero boundary failures. Not yet demonstrated: a confirmatory-scale ChartQA family, and any 2B Math result.
2. **Counterbalancing: [Verified] empirically justified on these stimuli.**
   - Order moves raw Δℓ by +1.5 to +2.0 nats/token toward the last source in Math (§8.2).
   - Order almost fixes the stated verdict when the text comes first (§7.2).
   - Order also confounds the direction of the flip: in `text_first`, own is almost always text, so flips are almost all text→image.
   - Caveat: in Math, order and flip direction are therefore partly confounded within the CDE.
3. **2B Math eligibility: [Verified] a parser and format issue.** It is not an accuracy conclusion. Δℓ itself would be computable for 2B.

   *Decision:* whether competency for 2B (or all models) may use a different format rule. Changing it after seeing dev data has to be logged as a deviation, applied to all models, and decided before the freeze.
4. **ChartQA feasibility: [Inferred] the confirmatory ChartQA family is very unlikely to reach 100 items for any model** at the dev rates. Competency alone (≈ 43%) caps 3B and 7B near 86 of 199, before any gate attrition. The dev n of 30 makes these rates imprecise.

   *Decisions:* the `max_pixels` rerun (R8.4); whether ChartQA is pre-declared secondary; how to treat the unimplemented §8 60% gate, which as written would stop the run.
5. **Wording-floor failure: [Verified]** 20–28% churn in Math. Under the preregistration the behavioural TOST is uninterpretable for Math (§7.6, line 406, which anticipated exactly this). An H₀ reading is unavailable in Math under the current rules. This does not affect the validity of Δℓ. Two related limits on the secondary outcome:
   - The 7B "neither" rate (62.7%) and its 1,610 fallback-parsed `beh` rows mean the strict behavioural outcome is mostly non-attributable for 7B.
   - ChartQA's answer-only decodes are stable but very small samples.
6. **ρ > 1: [Verified] mathematically valid.** ρ is a ratio of two independent mean contrasts: verdict swap vs explicit-instruction swap. Nothing bounds the first by the second.
   - What ρ > 1 says: in this prefix and at the `####` position, swapping the verdict noun moves the answer-token margin more than swapping the explicit-instruction target does.
   - What it does not say: that the verdict "decides" more than an instruction in behaviour, that the effect is a proportion, or that it generalizes.
   - For 3B the denominator is small (0.15) and comes **entirely** from the text direction, while 79% of its oriented CDE cells (192 + 316 of 640) are text→image flips. ρ_3B is therefore partly a direction mismatch.
   - The prereg reports the ceiling per direction (§4.3) but defines ρ with the total ceiling.
   - *Decision (pre-freeze):* whether ρ, or its reading, should be direction-matched. This changes R4/R6, which the preregistration says dev numbers may not change. That conflict itself needs an explicit, logged decision.
7. **Explicit ceilings: [Verified] they function in 7B Math and in ChartQA at the Δℓ level, but only half-function in 3B Math.**
   - At the Δℓ level, 3B Math `explicit_image` is about equal to the non-binding baseline.
   - Behaviourally, 3B Math `explicit_image` does raise image answers (68 vs about 28–30 under non-binding own/none).
   - Behaviourally, ChartQA `explicit_text` is weak (3B: 1/12 report answers).
   - [Hypothesis] For Math, Δℓ is scored at `####` immediately after the verdict, with no working. The 3B answer-token prior there may track order and surface cues more than the instructed problem. The Δℓ–behaviour dissociation in 3B is consistent with that, but it was not tested.

### 10.2 Remaining threats

- **Provenance:** most rows were computed in sessions with no stored transcript or manifest state. Library versions in those sessions are [Unknown]. The installs are unpinned. `resolved_model_commit` is null.
- **Label validity:** `stated_reliable_source` is unvalidated (§8 human coding is not done), and it has at least one demonstrated mislabel ("visual"). Every competency, gate, orientation and manipulation number depends on it.
- **Selection:** the both-orders requirement and the gate-3b rule select for terse, single-noun verdicts. The 3B and 7B competent sets differ (intersection 54 of 80 and 124).
- **Statistical:** sign-flip p-values sit at the resolution floor of 1/10,001. Bootstrap CIs with n = 2 or 6 are uninformative. Holm is applied inside groups that the preregistration would not admit to a family.
- **Code/design gaps:** the §8 60% gate is not implemented. `reading_binding` ignores `meets_min_items`. The size comparison has no minimum n. Boundary-mismatch ≤ 1% is reported but not enforced.
- **Freeze record (R8.3):** the following are still missing:
  - a stored analysis output;
  - the ChartQA render gate;
  - the `max_pixels` decision;
  - human coding;
  - an explicit statement of the three-way competency intersection (Math 0, ChartQA 8).
